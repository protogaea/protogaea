# 0009. The name: Protogaea

- **Status:** Accepted, pending a legal check
- **Date:** 2026-09-25
- **Specification:** §26, §31

## Context

The working name was "Pangea". A preliminary check on 2026-09-24 (not a legal opinion) found it to be a high-risk brand:

- Steam has at least four games with "Pangea" in the title, and there are board games about Pangea.
- In the EU, the marks "Lands Of Pangea" and "PANGEAN GAMES" are registered in the video-game classes; the second also covers crypto and NFTs.
- Crypto projects use the name — for example, the Pangea Swap exchange and its STONE token — which is especially bad for a project that promises "no token".
- The obvious domains and the `pangea` GitHub login are taken.

In Russia, the marks «ПАНГЕЯ» and "PANGEA" belong to a company in unrelated classes (instruments, geological exploration), so the risk there is low.

## Decision

The project and the world are called **Protogaea** (Russian «Протогея», "proto-Earth"; also the title of Leibniz's treatise on the early Earth). **Pangea** remains the name of the Season 1 supercontinent, and the season is titled "The Breaking of Pangea".

## Consequences

- The name fits every future season. Pangea is chapter one; an ice age or an archipelago would not fit under "Pangea".
- No marks or products named Protogaea were found; `protogaea.com` and the `protogaea` GitHub login were free at the time of the check.
- The name is harder to pronounce in English, and spelling variants must be secured (`Protogea` is already taken on GitHub).
- Before the repository is published: a lawyer checks the name in classes 9, 41 and 42 (and 28, if there is merchandise) in the US, the EU, the UK and Russia, and the domains and namespaces are registered.

## Alternatives considered

- **Pangea** — high risk; see above.
- **Pangea Wilds, Pangea Drift** — available, but the EU trademark risk remains.
- **Lifedrift** — no marks found, but the `.com` domain is taken.
- **Vivagaea, Urgaea** — hard to pronounce, or the GitHub name is taken.
- **Ediacara, Pangenesis, Panthalassa, Rodinia** — existing trademarks or games.
