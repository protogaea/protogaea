# Trademark policy (draft)

> **Draft.** A lawyer will review this policy before the repository is published ([spec §26](docs/spec/spec-v0.2.md#26-openness-licenses-and-language) and [§31](docs/spec/spec-v0.2.md#31-decisions-to-make-before-writing-code)). It is not legal advice.

"Protogaea" and the Protogaea logo (once it exists) identify this project and its official world. The code is open source; the name is not.

## Why the name is protected

- Naturalists must be able to tell the official world from forks — especially forks that add a token, which Protogaea will never have.
- The trust model depends on knowing whose log you are verifying.

## What you may do without asking

- Refer to Protogaea by name in articles, videos, research papers, talks and reviews.
- Say that your software "works with Protogaea" or is "compatible with the Protogaea protocol", if that is true.
- Use the name when redistributing unmodified official releases.
- Say that your world "runs on Protogaea software", if it does.

## What you may not do

- Use "Protogaea", or a confusingly similar name, as the name of a fork, a modified world, a product or a company.
- Use the name or logo in connection with any token, coin, NFT, "play-to-earn" scheme or sale of influence.
- Suggest that your fork, world or service is official or endorsed by the project.
- Register domains, handles or package names that imitate the project.

## Forks

Forks are welcome — that is what open source is for. Please give your fork a different name and make it clear that it is not the official Protogaea world.

Season titles and in-world names, such as Pangea and "The Breaking of Pangea", are part of the world's fiction, not trademarks. Forks may use them as long as they do not imply that they are the official world.

## If the project stops

Under the world handover plan (spec §26), if the operator stops supporting the world, it will publish the final archive and allow the community to continue the world from the last snapshot, including under the project's name. The exact terms will be defined together with this policy.
